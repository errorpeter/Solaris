# 🌕 SOLARIS — Lunar Emergency Mission Simulator

**A space-based emergency simulation game inspired by NASA's Artemis lunar exploration program.**

SOLARIS is an interactive, educational 2D simulation game designed to help young learners understand the challenges of surviving and making critical decisions during extreme solar storms on the Moon.

Set at NASA's Artemis Base Camp near the lunar South Pole, players take on the role of an astronaut commander responsible for protecting the crew and managing limited resources when a powerful Coronal Mass Ejection (CME) threatens the mission.

## 🚀 Project Overview

Imagine you're living on the Moon when an extreme solar storm is approaching. You have limited time, limited energy, and the safety of your crew depends on your decisions.

What would you do?

In SOLARIS, players must respond to an incoming solar storm by making strategic decisions, managing resources, and balancing astronaut safety with mission objectives.

## 🎮 Key Features

- **Emergency Simulation:** Experience a lunar emergency triggered by an incoming solar storm.
- **Resource Management:** Make strategic decisions involving energy, radiation protection, shielding, and mission resources.
- **Decision-Based Gameplay:** Every choice influences mission progress and astronaut safety.
- **Educational Experience:** Learn about solar activity, radiation hazards, and lunar exploration through interactive gameplay.
- **Multiple Mission Outcomes:** Your decisions determine whether you achieve mission success, partial success, or mission abort.

## 🌌 Mission Environment

The game is inspired by NASA's Artemis program and the concept of establishing a sustainable human presence on the Moon.

Players operate in a simulated lunar environment, where they must respond to space weather threats while protecting astronauts and preserving essential mission resources.

## 🎯 Target Audience

SOLARIS is designed primarily for students aged **7–12**, introducing complex space science concepts through accessible and engaging gameplay.

## 🛰️ Data & Research Sources

This project draws inspiration from publicly available space science resources and NASA datasets, including:

- [NASA DONKI](https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/tools/DONKI/) — Space weather events and solar activity information.
- [NASA Open Science Data Repository (OSDR)](https://osdr.nasa.gov/) — Open scientific research data.
- [NASA Artemis Program](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/artemis/) — Information about lunar exploration and future human missions.

## 🏆 Project Background

SOLARIS was developed as part of the **NASA International Space Apps Challenge**, under the challenge:

*Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer.*

The project aims to transform space science concepts into an interactive learning experience, encouraging young learners to explore, think critically, and make decisions like real astronauts.
